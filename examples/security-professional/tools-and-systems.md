## CIO-ISSO KPI Set (Role-Specific)

### Risk and Compliance

- **ATO Milestone Attainment:** % of planned ATO package milestones completed on schedule.
- **POA&M Closure Rate:** % of POA&M items closed within target SLA by risk tier.
- **Control Effectiveness Score:** pass rate for key controls during monthly/quarterly assessments.

### Vulnerability and Threat Posture

- **Critical/High Remediation SLA:** % of vulnerabilities remediated within required timelines.
- **Aging High-Risk Backlog:** count of open critical/high findings older than threshold.
- **Detection-to-Containment Time:** median time from alert triage to containment action.

### Identity and Access Governance

- **Access Review Completion:** % of required recertifications completed by due date.
- **Separation-of-Duties Exceptions:** total open SoD conflicts and overdue exception reviews.
- **Provisioning Cycle Time:** median business days from approved request to access enablement.

### Change and Modernization Governance

- **Secure Change Success Rate:** % of changes implemented without security rollback/incident.
- **TRB Security Gate Pass Rate:** % of initiatives passing security architecture review on first submission.
- **Shadow Workflow Reduction:** count of decommissioned ungoverned tools/workflows handling financial data.

## CIO-ISSO Dashboard Layout (Executive + Operational Views)

### 1) Executive Overview (Top Row)

- Overall risk scorecard (red/amber/green) by major system.
- ATO/POA&M status snapshot with trend arrows.
- Audit findings burn-down and overdue count.

### 2) Access Governance Panel

- Recertification completion by bureau/process owner.
- SoD conflict heatmap by application and role family.
- Provisioning and deprovisioning SLA trend lines.

### 3) Vulnerability and Incident Panel

- Critical/high findings by age bucket.
- Patch/remediation SLA attainment by system owner.
- Detection, response, and containment cycle-time trends.

### 4) Change Control and Modernization Panel

- TRB pipeline: submitted, approved, conditioned, rejected.
- Change success/failure and rollback metrics.
- RPA/AI use-case inventory with control status and residual risk flag.

### 5) QMS and Continuous Improvement Panel

- KPI performance vs target (monthly and quarterly).
- VOC themes mapped to corrective/preventive actions.
- Process nonconformance count and closure timeliness.

### 6) Data Governance and Evidence Panel

- Logging coverage and retention compliance.
- Evidence package readiness by audit domain.
- Data quality checks for reporting completeness and timeliness.
