# Goals and Priorities

## CIO-ISSO Top 10 Priorities (CGFS)

1. Maintain and continuously improve ATO posture across GFMS/RFMS/Phoenix, GLAAS, GFACS, and connected financial/admin systems.
2. Enforce least-privilege and separation-of-duties controls for all privileged and finance-impacting roles.
3. Institutionalize quarterly access recertification with documented evidence and exception handling.
4. Reduce vulnerability remediation cycle time, prioritizing exploitable findings on internet-facing and high-value assets.
5. Drive POA&M closure discipline with risk-based aging thresholds and accountable owners.
6. Integrate security-by-design gates into TRB and SDLC workflows (requirements, design, test, deploy).
7. Apply secure modernization guardrails for FBS initiatives, including RPA and AI/StateChat usage boundaries.
8. Strengthen logging, monitoring, and audit trail completeness for financial decision-support workflows.
9. Align security performance management with ISO 9001 QMS process ownership and VOC feedback loops.
10. Eliminate shadow tooling and undocumented workflows that process CGFS financial data outside governed systems.

## Current Priorities

1. Sustain and improve control effectiveness across enterprise systems.
2. Maintain high audit readiness and continue improving compliance outcomes.
3. Strengthen IAM and PKI governance while improving user-access efficiency.
4. Expand automation to reduce response times and manual security overhead.
5. Build executive visibility through a standard CIO-ISSO KPI dashboard.

## Success Metrics

- Compliance gaps identified and remediated in defined timelines.
- Audit performance trend remains positive year over year.
- Access governance operations remain secure, timely, and consistent.
- Incident response and operational workflows show measurable cycle-time reduction.
- ATO and POA&M milestones are met with predictable month-over-month improvement.
- High-risk vulnerability backlog decreases quarter over quarter.

## Strategic Direction

- Continue maturing risk-based security operations tied to NIST 800-53.
- Improve standardization across global operations and user populations.
- Increase use of tooling and reporting to support faster, higher-quality decisions.

## Deliberate Non-Priorities (Right Now)

- Broad platform changes that do not improve security posture or audit outcomes.
- Low-impact process work that adds overhead without reducing risk.
