# Decision Log

## Decision Pattern

I make security and operational decisions by balancing risk reduction, compliance obligations, and delivery speed. I prioritize changes that are evidence-backed, repeatable, and measurable.

---

## Example 1: Control Remediation Prioritization Across Multiple Systems

**Context:** Multiple control findings were open across a broad system set.  
**Decision:** Prioritize remediation by risk impact, audit exposure, and dependency constraints instead of FIFO closure.  
**Why:** This approach maximized overall risk reduction and improved audit readiness faster.  
**Outcome:** More efficient closure sequencing and improved compliance posture over time.

---

## Example 2: IAM Process Standardization for Distributed Users

**Context:** Access operations served users across multiple international locations with varying operational timing needs.  
**Decision:** Standardize core access governance steps while preserving clear escalation paths for urgent mission needs.  
**Why:** Consistency improves control quality and auditability; escalation preserves operational continuity.  
**Outcome:** Improved provisioning reliability and stronger governance consistency.

---

## Example 3: Security Tooling Adoption for Response Efficiency

**Context:** Incident and compliance workflows included manual bottlenecks that slowed turnaround.  
**Decision:** Expand use of PowerShell and Splunk for repeatable data collection and operational triage.  
**Why:** Automation reduced cycle time and improved repeatability of security operations.  
**Outcome:** Response times improved and reporting quality became more consistent.
