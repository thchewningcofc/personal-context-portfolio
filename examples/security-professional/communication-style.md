# Communication Style

## How I Prefer to Receive Information

- Direct, concise summaries first, with technical detail available immediately after.
- Evidence-based recommendations tied to risk, compliance, and operational impact.
- Clear action items with owners, due dates, and expected outcomes.

## How I Prefer Deliverables to Be Written

- Executive summary up front.
- Structured sections for findings, impact, remediation plan, and status.
- Measurable metrics where possible (efficiency, audit scores, incident rates).
- Language that is precise enough for auditors and practical enough for operators.

## Collaboration Preferences

- Early alignment when control requirements may affect implementation timelines.
- Transparent escalation on blockers that create security or audit risk.
- Consistent follow-through on remediation and access-governance commitments.

## What to Avoid

- Vague recommendations without control mapping or measurable outcomes.
- Long narrative updates with no ownership or deadline clarity.
- Last-minute requests that could have been surfaced earlier in the cycle.
