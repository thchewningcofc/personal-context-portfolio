# Team and Relationships

## Core Internal Partners

- **Security leadership and compliance stakeholders:** Align on control posture, audit readiness, and risk priorities.
- **System owners and technical teams:** Implement remediation plans and validate control effectiveness.
- **IAM and PKI operators:** Coordinate access governance and certificate lifecycle activities.
- **Incident response stakeholders (CIRT/operations):** Manage response workflows and follow-up actions.

## External / Cross-Functional Relationships

- **Audit and oversight stakeholders:** Provide evidence, control documentation, and remediation status.
- **Global site contacts (Charleston, Bangkok, Paris):** Coordinate access and operational consistency across locations.

## How I Typically Interact

- Security findings and remediation alignment through structured status updates.
- Operational coordination through ticketing systems and defined workflows.
- Metrics-driven updates that focus on risk reduction and measurable outcomes.

## What These Partners Need From Me

- Clear interpretation of security controls and compliance requirements.
- Practical remediation guidance that fits operational realities.
- Timely response on access governance and incident-related decisions.
- Reliable audit artifacts and defensible security documentation.
